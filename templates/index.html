Why Votora?

More than a Google Form.

Running a society election on a survey tool means trusting that nobody submits twice, that ranked ballots get counted correctly, and that the executive team can actually run the meeting without a spreadsheet meltdown. Votora is a free online voting platform built to solve exactly that — for AGMs, council elections, and motions that need proper rules, not just a poll link in the group chat.

Votora gives you QR check-in, one ballot per member, and automated tallying for preference, cumulative, score, and yes/no votes. It is independent, purpose-built, and free. If you are looking for something fairer than a Form and simpler than enterprise election software, you are in the right place.
